# Orkestra: golden yellow and cobalt

Supersedes the V2 petrol/copper palette. The five-circle logo geometry is unchanged.

## Palette

- Golden yellow `#FFDD00`: hero surface, inverse mark and selected brand accents.
- Cobalt `#1246B5`: logo on light surfaces, headings, primary buttons and footer.
- Parchment `#FAF8F5`: content and form surfaces.
- Ink `#17233B`: long-form text.
- Cobalt hover `#0C338C`: interaction and footer depth.

Primary and inverse logos are single-color so no satellite disappears against the hero. Normal-size text pairings are checked against a 4.5:1 contrast threshold in `brandPalette.test.ts`. Warning/error colors retain their semantic meaning.

## Typography and voice

Outfit display text uses a lighter weight and shorter phrases, inspired by the supplied geometric reference rather than claiming to reproduce its custom lettering. Geist remains the body font. Turkish glyph support and the reduced-motion fallback are preserved.

The primary message is “İşini anlat. Doğru ustayla buluş.” Search, categories and the existing wizard remain functional. Transactional surfaces stay light; yellow does not become the background of every form.

## Scope

Updated shared tokens, hero/header/footer, category introduction, card elevation, receipt branding and favicon. Social-share artwork and historical concept screens are not part of this local visual iteration. Real-device visual QA has not been performed.
